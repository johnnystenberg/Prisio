Sample price tags — scan without a store.

Prisio reads the price on the live camera feed and converts it to your home currency (in the US/Canada it also adds local sales tax). You can test it right here — no real shelf needed.

  1. Tap a tag below to enlarge it on this screen.
  2. In Prisio, point the camera at the enlarged price — it converts automatically (no shutter).
  3. To change the destination, open Settings → Region in the app (a US region adds sales tax; a European one shows a tax-inclusive price).
